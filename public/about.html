Free. No account, no email, no app store. Works on any phone.

Every chat app has a toolbar. The problem is what's in it.

Open Messenger and you get games nobody plays, a GIF search that never finds the one you want, and stickers drawn for somebody else. It is a big catalogue, and that is exactly the problem: it is not yours.

Your group reuses about twenty GIFs. Searching a library of millions to find one of them is a tax you pay for not owning it. Twenty does not need a search box at all.

🎒

Bring your own emoji

Make them with AI, draw them, photograph them. Drop them in and type :kev:. Everyone in the group sees them, whether or not they installed anything.

📦

Packs you can take away

A pack is an ordinary zip file. Export it, email it, keep it for ten years, load it into another group. No other chat app lets you walk out with your emoji.

🔁

A GIF library that fills itself

Every GIF anyone sends is there next time, newest first, searchable. No saving, no faffing. The ones you actually use, not a catalogue of millions.

Made in an afternoon, by the group

Ask any AI image tool for a sheet of faces in one style, drop them in, name them. Now your group has emoji that mean something to the eight of you and nothing to anybody else. That is the point.

A pack is just a zip

No proprietary format, no lock-in, nothing to sign up for. An entry either carries its picture or points at one that already lives somewhere else, which is why a GIF pack is a few hundred bytes and costs nobody anything to keep.

friday-crew.zip
├── pack.json
├── kev.webp
└── chicken.gif

{
  "format": "purepack/1",
  "name": "Friday crew",
  "emoji": [
    { "name": "kev",     "file": "kev.webp" },
    { "name": "chicken", "file": "chicken.gif" },
    { "name": "classic", "url":  "https://example.com/classic.gif" }
  ]
}

Where it is up to

Pure Chat is early, and it is better to say so. It is in daily use by its own small group, it is free to run, and these are the honest limits today:

  • No accounts. You type a name. Anyone with the invite link can join, and there is no way to remove them afterwards.
  • Not private from us. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not put anything in it you would mind being read.
  • No notifications yet. You find out when you open it.
  • Nothing is guaranteed. It is a side project on free infrastructure, not a company.

Good for a group of friends messing about. Not yet good for anything that matters.
